# Preparing your business location project

Before looking for land or business premises, a few simple questions help you save time and have better discussions with territories.

![illustration of a business park](/preparer-son-implantation/cover.svg)

## Define your needs

A location project is first described by its constraints. The more precise they are, the easier it is to identify suitable listings:

- **Area**: land area, floor area and mid-term expansion needs.

- **Access**: proximity to a highway, a train station or a port, and heavy goods vehicle access.

- **Utilities**: electrical capacity, water, sewerage and fiber optics.

- **Timeline**: target move-in date and administrative steps to anticipate.

## Identify the right territories

Once your needs are defined, you can focus your search on the territories that meet them. Business land listings are most often provided by local authorities and their economic development agencies, who know which sites are actually available.
