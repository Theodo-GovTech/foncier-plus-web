# Foncier+: The public-sector network dedicated to boosting the economic appeal of your areas

![photo of a business development site](/collectivites/ECO_815.jpg)

## From search engine to shared governance

Launched in April 2024 as a joint initiative between the French State, Caisse des Dépôts and Cerema, the France Foncier+ portal has quickly established itself as the national showcase for business development land, with more than 1,000 sites listed and more than 8,000 hectares identified across France.

Responding to the increasing scarcity of land, meeting sustainability targets (Zero Net Artificialisation) and the need to accelerate reindustrialisation meant that something more than a simple shop window was needed. So to scale up its operations and deliver a direct response to grassroots demand, France Foncier+ is now Foncier+ and has been restructured as a non-profit public interest organisation.

What’s changing for your local authority:

- **Service continuity guaranteed:** Your inventory tools (e.g. ZAE business development zone opportunities) and business development land listings remain fully active and guaranteed.

- **From use to co-construction:** Foncier+ now does much more than simply display your data. Its non-profit public interest organisation status provides a stable legal and operational framework for coordinating institutions, local authorities, public-sector bodies and businesses.

- **A central role in governance:** Joining the Collège des Collectivités Territoriales (Local & Regional Authorities’ Council) gives you an active role in the strategic decision-making of Foncier+. You can then guide the prioritisation of features and data density to reflect your own local requirements, and maximise your impact on the decisions companies make about new development locations.

## Our Foncier+ ambitions

Foncier+ has been designed to support the work of local authorities (regions, local authority partnerships, urban centres and development agencies) without ever seeking to replace local guidance. Our ambition has its roots in three key commitments:

### 1. To qualify enquiries more effectively and maximise the potential of your land bank

Becoming the go-to source for your business development land use data. As well as showcasing available sites, Foncier+ helps you pinpoint your land-use opportunities and boost their potential at an early stage. Making your data more reliable and more comprehensive (to include utilities, water, electricity, development planning and ICPEs (installations classified for environmental protection) paves the way for maximising future opportunities and supporting informed decision-making by industry and investors.

### 2. To offer complementary tools and a national showcase

**Zero re-entry of data.** Foncier+ integrates with your existing tools and GIS, as long as they use common data standards tailored to the needs of businesses. You can upload data to the national platform without having to enter the same information twice.

**Closer coordination with national and local ecosystems.** Foncier+ bridges the gap between local initiatives and major national promoters of inward investment in France, including Business France and Territoires d’Industrie. We make it easy for you to share information with the full range of partners, without compromising your local or regional governance structures and without ever seeking to take their place.

### 3. To ensure qualified referrals and provide support at every stage of project implementation

**A much higher profile.** The optimised Foncier+ digital platform maximises the profile of your business development land opportunities in France and internationally.

**The ‘Response to every project’ commitment.** Foncier+ aims to ensure that every enquiry is followed up in conjunction with your own services so that enquiries that proved unsuccessful in one region can be redirected to other regions.

**A sector-based approach.** Targeted pre-qualification processes to ensure that only fully developed projects compatible with the characteristics of your development sites are passed on to your teams. Foncier+ monitors every site application in close collaboration with your own services.

**Reporting and monitoring.** End-to-end information sharing to track the progress of previously identified development projects in your area.

## Joining Foncier+

Membership of Foncier+ is free and open to all local authorities, EPCI (public establishments for intercommunal cooperation), development agencies and other public bodies committed to resource efficiency and economic development.

A 4-step process:

1. **Application:** Membership applications should be submitted to the President and Chief Executive Officer, accompanied by details of your legal representative (or a resolution from your executive committee authorising membership).

2. **Review by the Management Board:** Validation of the application and official affiliation with Council 4 – Local & Regional Authorities.

3. **Notification and Membership Agreement:** The decision of the Management Board will be accompanied by a membership agreement to be signed by both parties. This agreement sets out our mutual commitments (roles and responsibilities for data monitoring and updating, data update frequency and target response times for referrals).

4. **Operational introduction:** Our teams provide you with the tailormade support needed to help you monitor your business development land portfolio and promote your opportunities effectively (either by integrating your tools or managing your opportunities directly).

5. **Integration into governance and co-construction:** Active participation in the Collège des Collectivités territoriales. You have the opportunity to contribute directly to the work of Foncier+, the development of standards and platform-related strategic choices as part of ensuring that all these are tailored as closely as possible to needs at grassroots level.

**Maximise the potential of your business development land.** Whether you want to compile an inventory of your ZAE business development zone opportunities, list your business development land opportunities to attract investors or become a member of Foncier+, our teams are here to support you every step of the way.
