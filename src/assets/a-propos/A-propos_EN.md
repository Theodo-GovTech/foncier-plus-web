# About Foncier+: connecting business location projects with territories

From France Foncier+ to Foncier+: discover the mission, evolution, and operations of the association serving business setup projects.

![picture of an industrial site](/a-propos/ECO_454.svg)

## From a national portal to a matchmaking service

Finding available land is not enough. You still need to know if it fits your project, identify the right contact, and be able to take the next steps.

To make this search clearer nationwide, **France Foncier+** was launched in 2024 at the initiative of Banque des Territoires and Cerema. The portal lists commercial and industrial land options provided by local authorities and facilitates contact with local stakeholders.

Since its launch, the service has helped build an initial national foundation:

- **Over 1,000 listed sites** representing more than 8,000 hectares.

- **Over 40,000 visits** recorded since launch.

- **Over 250 inquiries** forwarded to regional stakeholders since launch.

## From France Foncier+ to Foncier+: scaling up to connect better

France Foncier+ demonstrated the value of a single national entry point. Foncier+ builds on this usefulness with a broader ambition: transforming land listing visibility into simpler, higher-quality connections. The initiative is now taking the form of an **association: Foncier+**, designed to gradually unite territories and partners around a shared service.

Foncier+ is becoming the core brand behind this initiative. The goal is no longer just making listings visible, but helping every project identify the right territory and contact to move forward with.

**What changes with Foncier+:**

- A national single entry point to search for commercial/industrial land and identify local contacts.

- A gradual pooling of data and tools, built on open data and open source principles.

A guiding principle: **No project left without an answer.** Foncier+ can step in as a relay whenever a search or connection falls through.

## An association built alongside territories

Spearheaded by Banque des Territoires with the support of the Ministry of Industry, **Foncier+** aims to bring together local authorities, economic development agencies, national operators, and business representatives around a common national service.

- Local authorities, intercommunal bodies (EPCIs), and their economic development agencies play a central role: they know the land offer, qualify listings, and support projects on the ground.

- National operators and networks can contribute through their data, expertise, and ability to reach out to businesses.

- Business representatives help share project leaders' needs, constraints, and location criteria.

This collective governance is designed to progressively improve data quality, listing visibility, and connection efficiency, without replacing local support.
